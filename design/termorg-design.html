What you're seeing

Four terminals across two project groups. The agent tile (cream border) is selected; its pulsing ✱ bubbles up into the sidebar so attention is legible without scanning every tile.

Selection vs. focus

One tile is "current" — that's selection. Pressing ⏎ drops into focused mode on it. Selection is also where m moves from when reorganizing groups.

Density choice

Tiles render the last screenful of each terminal. Truncation is silent and consistent — no fade-out, no "show more" affordance. Full scrollback lives in focused mode.

Group accents

Earth-tones, not categorical primaries: ochre, sage, terracotta, slate. Hand-picked to coexist without competing — a project palette, not a category legend.

Status, four states

Active ● · attention ✱ · error ✗ · quiet ·. Each terminal and group inherits the loudest state of its children.

What changes

Sidebar dims to ~40% opacity but stays mouse-live — clicking another terminal switches focus without leaving focused mode. The mode pill flips to FOCUS in amber. The bottom bar swaps cockpit commands for prefix-key reminders.

Passthrough

Every keystroke, mouse event, and ANSI sequence flows to the PTY. The inner program (here, Claude Code) owns the screen — colors, cursor position, scrollback. We just render what its vt100 stream says.

Why a prefix exists

Inner programs eat Esc and most modifiers. Ctrl-Space is reserved as the one key we capture before anything else — your way out. Configurable; the default is a deliberate choice (rarely bound elsewhere).

Caret

The blinking block is ours, drawn over whatever the inner program is doing. It's a liveness signal: input is flowing, we're alive, you're connected.
